The Yokohama ADVAN V03 (often listed locally as V03A) 225/65R17 is an Original Equipment (OE) luxury touring and SUV/MPV tire engineered in Japan by Yokohama Rubber Co., Ltd. for cabin quietness, ride comfort, and high-speed stability. Here is the complete specification and product description in English, structured for reference or direct use in product listings: 1. Basic Specifications • Brand: Yokohama • Model / Pattern: ADVAN V03 / V03A • Tire Size: 225/65R17 (225 mm section width, 65% aspect ratio, 17-inch rim diameter) • Load & Speed Index: 102H (Max load capacity of 850 kg per tire; maximum speed rating of 210 km/h) • Category: Luxury Touring / Urban SUV & MPV Summer Tire • Country of Origin: Japan (Made in Japan) 2. Core Technology & Performance Features • Factory OE Engineering: Originally developed as factory-fitted Original Equipment for the Toyota Alphard and Vellfire (40-Series), featuring a reinforced internal structure tailored to support the heavier curb weight and higher center of gravity of luxury MPVs and SUVs. • AI-Optimized Acoustic Comfort: Engineered using Yokohama's HAICoLab AI development framework to optimize tread pitch sequencing, effectively breaking up road resonance and minimizing cabin noise. • Superior Shock Absorption: A specialized tread compound and tuned sidewall construction absorb minor road imperfections, delivering a smooth, composed ride on both city roads and highways. • Wet & Dry Stability: Four wide circumferential grooves efficiently evacuate water to resist hydroplaning, while high-rigidity shoulder blocks enhance cornering stability and steering response. 3. Common Vehicle Compatibility (Malaysia) Beyond its factory fitment on the Toyota Alphard and Vellfire, 225/65R17 is a direct OE fitment for several popular SUVs and crossovers in Malaysia: • Toyota: Harrier, RAV4 • Honda: CR-V • Mazda: CX-5 • Proton: X70 • Subaru: Forester 4. Local Market & Buying Notes (Malaysia) • Meaning of the "V03A" Suffix: The "A" stamped on the sidewall is an OE manufacturer specification code indicating the specific rubber compound and construction approved for factory vehicle assembly. • Typical Stock Sources: Because the ADVAN V03/V03A is produced primarily for vehicle assembly plants, stock sold at local Malaysian tire workshops (such as in Klang and Shah Alam) generally comes as either parallel-imported brand-new Japanese stock or new-car take-offs (tires removed at delivery mileage from imported reconditioned Alphards/Vellfires).
